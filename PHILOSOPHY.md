# Product Philosophy

Explain.fm treats explanation as a designed learning experience. Good explanations begin with the listener's context, expose the shape of a problem, connect new ideas to familiar ones, and make uncertainty visible.

Conversation provides pacing, contrast, questions, and repair when an idea is difficult. Story provides motivation and memory. Sources provide accountability. Evaluation closes the loop.

Automation is valuable only when it improves those qualities. Human review and explicit evidence remain appropriate wherever automated confidence exceeds actual knowledge.
