# Project

Explain.fm is an AI-native Knowledge Storytelling Platform that transforms trusted sources into natural podcast-style conversations.

## Product outcome

A listener chooses a topic and receives an accurate, natural episode that can be followed while working without looking at a screen. Quizzes and comprehension tests are optional tools, not part of the listening experience or a release requirement.

## Product boundary

Explain.fm is not a generic AI podcast generator, a summarizer, or a chatbot. Its core unit is an evidence-grounded explanation shaped for listening and retention.

## Current phase

The project is archived as of 2026-07-02 after the Era 4 personal-listening prototype failed its complete-episode review. No further implementation is active.
