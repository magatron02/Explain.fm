# Project

Explain.fm is an AI-native Knowledge Storytelling Platform that transforms trusted sources into natural podcast-style conversations.

## Product outcome

A listener should finish an episode with an accurate mental model: what the topic is, why it matters, how it works, which examples make it concrete, and where the boundaries of knowledge lie.

## Product boundary

Explain.fm is not a generic AI podcast generator, a summarizer, or a chatbot. Its core unit is an evidence-grounded explanation shaped for listening and retention.

## Current phase

Era 2 — Story is active. The DNS research brief, story plan, script, and complete VoxCPM2 audio prototype pass their automated gates. Era 2 remains open until the 5-minute episode passes human listening review.
