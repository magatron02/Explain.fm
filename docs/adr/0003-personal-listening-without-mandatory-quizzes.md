# ADR 0003: Personal listening without mandatory quizzes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-02

## Context

Explain.fm is a personal podcast for topics the user chooses and listens to while working. A required comprehension study turns listening into coursework and blocks delivery without improving that core use case.

## Decision

The user explicitly chooses the topic and may provide presentation preferences. Release gates cover source fidelity, factual accuracy, story quality, and listenable audio. Quizzes and listener studies are optional evaluation methods, never required product steps. Implicit profiling remains out of scope.

## Consequences

Era 4 exits through one accepted topic-to-audio run rather than cohort evidence. Historical experiments remain as evidence, but the unused cohort-study packet and decision tool are removed.
