# First Principles

1. People are overwhelmed by information.
2. People remember stories better than summaries.
3. People learn better from conversations than lectures.
4. Technology exists to serve understanding.
5. Every feature must improve understanding, retention, accessibility, or enjoyment.

When principles conflict, accuracy and understanding take precedence over speed, novelty, volume, and entertainment. A feature that cannot explain which principle it advances should not be built.
