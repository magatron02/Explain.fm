# Research Guide

## Objective

Produce a bounded, traceable research brief that a story planner can use without repeating source discovery.

## Source order

Prefer official documentation, primary sources, standards, original research, and direct records. Use reputable secondary sources for context or interpretation. Treat tertiary material as a discovery aid, not final evidence.

## Required practices

- Define the research question and audience before collecting material.
- Record source identity, date, authority, and relevant limitations.
- Trace every material claim to supporting evidence.
- Separate sourced facts, reasoned interpretations, and opinions.
- Represent meaningful disagreement and uncertainty.
- Stop when additional material no longer changes the explanation materially.

Research output must follow `contracts/research.md`. Citation presence does not replace checking whether a source actually supports a claim.
