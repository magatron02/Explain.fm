# Quality

Every episode is evaluated across these criteria:

- **Accuracy:** claims are correct, bounded, and supported.
- **Naturalness:** dialogue sounds conversational without filler or artificial conflict.
- **Storytelling:** the episode creates motivation, progression, and a memorable explanatory arc.
- **Source quality:** evidence is authoritative, relevant, current enough, and traceable.
- **Listenability:** structure, pacing, wording, and audio are easy to follow without a screen.
- **Retention:** recaps, examples, and connections support durable recall.
- **Usefulness:** the listener gains a practical mental model or clearer judgment.

Accuracy and source quality are gates, not averages. Strong entertainment cannot compensate for unsupported material. Detailed scoring thresholds belong in `evaluation/rubrics/` after golden episodes establish useful baselines.
