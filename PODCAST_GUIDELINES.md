# Podcast Guidelines

The goal is not summarization. The goal is storytelling that produces accurate understanding.

Every episode should include:

1. **Hook** — create curiosity without clickbait or unsupported claims.
2. **Motivation** — establish why the topic matters to the listener.
3. **Problem** — define the question, tension, or misconception.
4. **Explanation** — build the core mental model in manageable steps.
5. **Examples** — make abstractions concrete and test understanding.
6. **Comparison** — distinguish the idea from nearby alternatives.
7. **Common mistakes** — surface predictable misunderstandings and limits.
8. **Recap** — reconnect the key ideas without repeating the full episode.
9. **Takeaway** — leave one useful, memorable conclusion.

Conversation should sound purposeful rather than improvised. Speakers may question, clarify, and disagree, but they must not manufacture conflict or trade accuracy for energy.
