# Error Standard

- Fail explicitly when accuracy, provenance, authorization, or required inputs are compromised.
- Classify errors by stage and stable category.
- Include an actionable message, affected artifact, retry safety, and preserved diagnostics.
- Distinguish invalid input, unavailable dependency, quality rejection, partial output, and internal failure.
- Never convert missing evidence into generated content or present partial work as complete.
- Preserve the original cause without exposing secrets or sensitive listener information.
