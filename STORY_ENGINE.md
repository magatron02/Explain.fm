# Story Engine

The story engine converts a research brief into an explanatory plan before script writing begins.

## Responsibilities

- Identify the listener's starting point and desired understanding.
- Select a central question, tension, or misconception.
- Order concepts so each new idea has sufficient context.
- Choose examples, comparisons, and mental models supported by evidence.
- Allocate uncertainty and caveats where they matter.
- Produce the required episode beats from `PODCAST_GUIDELINES.md`.

The engine must not invent claims, citations, characters, or experience. It may improve sequence and framing, but material factual additions must return to research.
