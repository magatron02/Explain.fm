# Audio Rubric v1

Use this rubric on a complete rendered episode. A short smoke sample cannot pass.

## Automated gates

- Audio covers every approved speaker turn in order.
- The artifact records script revision, renderer, model revision, configuration, speaker mapping, and generation status.
- The file opens, has a declared sample rate and channel layout, and contains no missing or corrupt segment.
- Text transformations and partial renders are explicitly reported.

## Listening gates

| Criterion | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Intelligibility | Every sentence can be understood without reading the script. |
| Meaning preservation | Delivery does not change emphasis enough to distort a factual claim or caveat. |
| Speaker identity | The two speakers remain distinguishable and internally consistent. |
| Pronunciation | DNS, TTL, AAAA, names, and technical terms are spoken correctly. |
| Naturalness | Timing, stress, and turn-taking sound purposeful rather than stitched together. |
| Listenability | Pace, silence, loudness, and transitions remain comfortable for the complete episode. |

Any failure requires revision. Audio cannot pass without a human listening review.
