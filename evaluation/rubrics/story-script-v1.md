# Story Plan and Script Rubric v1

Use this rubric on a story plan and its script before voice rendering. Accuracy and evidence fidelity are gates.

## Automated gates

- The upstream research brief is valid.
- All nine required episode beats are present.
- Every beat contains at least one valid accepted-source citation.
- The script has at least two speakers and references its story plan.

Any automated-gate failure rejects the artifact.

## Review criteria

| Criterion | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Spoken factual claims remain within the cited research evidence. |
| Evidence fidelity | Story framing, examples, and dialogue do not alter provenance, certainty, or scope. |
| Explanation arc | Concepts arrive in a causal teaching order with a clear listener change. |
| Naturalness | Speakers question, clarify, and respond without fake conflict or decorative banter. |
| Listenability | Sentences, terminology, pacing, and transitions can be followed without a screen. |
| Retention | The episode uses a concrete model, comparison, correction, recap, and memorable takeaway. |
| Scope | The script excludes unsupported adjacent topics and live operational claims. |

## Decision

- **Accepted for voice prototype:** automated gates and all criteria pass.
- **Revise:** a correctable criterion fails.
- **Rejected:** accuracy or evidence fidelity fails.

Passing this text rubric does not prove audio quality or listener comprehension.
