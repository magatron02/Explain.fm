# Playbook: New Source

1. Record ownership, authority, date, access method, license, and usage constraints.
2. Define the source's intended research value and known limitations.
3. Collect a small sample while preserving original provenance.
4. Validate cleaning against loss, distortion, duplication, and retained noise.
5. Test citation resolution from derived claim back to source evidence.
6. Review security, privacy, freshness, and update behavior.
7. Admit the source only after it passes the relevant quality checks.
8. Update existing topic pages before creating new ones, and link the source and derived pages in both directions.
9. Add accepted notes and new derived pages to `knowledge/index.md`.
10. Append the completed ingest outcome, contradictions, and remaining gaps to `knowledge/log.md`.

Do not ingest a source merely because collection is technically possible.
