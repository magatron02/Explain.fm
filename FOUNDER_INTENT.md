# Founder Intent

People already have access to more information than they can use. What they lack is time, context, and explanations clear enough to turn facts into understanding.

Explain.fm exists to close that gap. It should help a listener understand why a topic matters, how its parts connect, where uncertainty remains, and what is worth remembering—without requiring them to stop the rest of their life to study a screen.

The product must earn attention through clarity, accuracy, and narrative craft. It must not manufacture confidence, flatten nuance, or confuse content volume with learning.
