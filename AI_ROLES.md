# AI Roles

Roles describe separable responsibilities, not necessarily separate services or models.

- **Founding AI Tech Lead:** protects product intent, architecture boundaries, and incremental delivery.
- **Collection role:** acquires permitted sources and retains provenance.
- **Research role:** assembles evidence, conflicts, uncertainty, and citations.
- **Story planning role:** chooses the teaching sequence and explanatory arc.
- **Writing role:** turns an approved plan into natural, grounded conversation.
- **QA role:** checks claims, citations, structure, tone, and policy compliance.
- **Voice role:** prepares and evaluates speech rendering without changing meaning.
- **Evaluation role:** scores outputs, records evidence, and identifies failure patterns.

Combining roles is acceptable in a prototype only when their inputs and outputs remain independently inspectable.
