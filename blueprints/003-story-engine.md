# Blueprint 003: Story Engine

## Goal

Turn grounded research into a compelling teaching sequence that improves understanding and retention.

## Responsibilities

- Establish the central question and listener motivation.
- Order concepts and evidence into an explanatory arc.
- Select grounded examples, comparisons, and mental models.
- Place caveats and uncertainty where they affect understanding.

## Inputs

Research brief, listener context, episode objective, duration target, and podcast guidelines.

## Outputs

A story plan conforming to `contracts/story.md` with required episode beats and evidence references.

## Dependencies

Research contract, podcast guidelines, domain model, and quality criteria.

## Failure Cases

Summary-shaped output, missing motivation, unsupported framing, premature concepts, false conflict, or buried nuance.

## Future Ideas

Alternative teaching arcs, explicit retention hypotheses, and evaluated story-pattern recommendations.
