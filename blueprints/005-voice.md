# Blueprint 005: Voice

## Goal

Convert an approved script into clear, natural audio while preserving factual meaning and conversational intent.

## Responsibilities

- Validate voice-ready structure and pronunciation guidance.
- Render distinct, appropriate speaker delivery.
- Preserve wording or explicitly report transformations.
- Return timing and generation diagnostics for review.

## Inputs

Approved script, speaker definitions, pronunciation notes, pacing guidance, and rendering constraints.

## Outputs

Audio artifacts and metadata conforming to `contracts/voice.md`.

## Dependencies

Script output, VoxCPM capability validation, audio storage, and listenability evaluation.

## Failure Cases

Mispronunciation, speaker confusion, clipped audio, altered meaning, inconsistent loudness, unnatural pacing, or unreported generation failure.

## Future Ideas

Accessible pacing modes, pronunciation learning, multilingual voices, and controlled expressive direction.
