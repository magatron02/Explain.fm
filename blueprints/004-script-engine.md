# Blueprint 004: Script Engine

## Goal

Render an approved story plan as natural, accurate podcast dialogue without changing its evidence or intent.

## Responsibilities

- Express each story beat through purposeful conversation.
- Preserve claims, citations, uncertainty, and teaching order.
- Use questions, clarification, contrast, and recap naturally.
- Produce speech-ready speaker turns.

## Inputs

Approved story plan, research brief, speaker constraints, duration target, and style guidance.

## Outputs

A structured script with speaker turns, evidence links, pronunciation notes, and review flags.

## Dependencies

Story and research contracts, podcast guidelines, constitution, and QA criteria.

## Failure Cases

Invented facts, citation drift, repetitive banter, fake disagreement, unnatural exposition, or meaning changed for style.

## Future Ideas

Multiple conversation formats, controlled revision passes, and listener-level pacing variants.
