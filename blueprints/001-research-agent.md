# Blueprint 001: Research Agent

## Goal

Produce a traceable research brief that gives the story planner enough reliable context to explain a bounded topic.

## Responsibilities

- Translate an approved topic into focused research questions.
- Find and assess authoritative evidence.
- Separate facts, interpretations, disagreements, and unknowns.
- Attach source support to material claims.

## Inputs

Topic scope, intended audience, source collection, research constraints, and relevant prior knowledge.

## Outputs

A research brief conforming to `contracts/research.md`, including claims, evidence, citations, uncertainty, conflicts, and open questions.

## Dependencies

Clean source material, provenance metadata, retrieval capability, and the research guide.

## Failure Cases

Unsupported synthesis, citation mismatch, poor source authority, scope drift, hidden disagreement, or false certainty.

## Future Ideas

Claim-level provenance views, source diversity checks, and targeted expert-review queues.
