# Blueprint 006: Evaluation

## Goal

Produce actionable evidence about whether research, story, script, and audio meet Explain.fm quality standards.

## Responsibilities

- Apply versioned rubrics to inspectable artifacts.
- Gate accuracy and source quality independently from style.
- Record scores, evidence, failures, and reviewer context.
- Route findings to the stage capable of correcting them.

## Inputs

Pipeline artifacts, provenance, rubric version, benchmark expectations, and reviewer observations.

## Outputs

An evaluation report conforming to `contracts/evaluation.md`, with pass state and actionable findings.

## Dependencies

Quality criteria, golden episodes, benchmark sets, and observable stage outputs.

## Failure Cases

Unsupported scores, rubric drift, aggregate scores hiding critical failures, contaminated benchmarks, or non-actionable feedback.

## Future Ideas

Listener comprehension studies, calibrated automated checks, regression dashboards, and inter-rater reliability tracking.
