# Domain Model

The initial domain language is conceptual and technology-neutral.

- **Source:** an origin artifact with provenance, authority, date, and usage constraints.
- **Evidence:** a traceable source passage or fact supporting a claim.
- **Concept:** an idea worth explaining or remembering.
- **Relationship:** a meaningful connection between concepts.
- **Insight:** a supported interpretation derived from evidence.
- **Research Brief:** scoped findings, claims, citations, uncertainty, and open questions.
- **Story Plan:** the explanatory arc and teaching sequence for an episode.
- **Script:** the speaker-by-speaker conversational representation of that plan.
- **Episode:** the published audio experience plus metadata and provenance.
- **Listener Profile:** explicit preferences and learning context, never inferred beyond available evidence.
- **Evaluation:** a recorded assessment against defined quality criteria.

Identifiers and schemas are deliberately deferred until a prototype proves the necessary boundaries.
